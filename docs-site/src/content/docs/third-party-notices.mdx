## Toolkit integrations

:::note[No distributed toolkit integrations yet]
Azure Data Lab Toolkit has no released provider, sample database, community tool, or software integration. Projects named in the community pages are planned candidates and retain their own ownership and terms.
:::

Future releases will list every bundled, downloaded, generated, or redistributed artifact with its version, owner, source, license, attribution, and material restrictions.

See [Licensing](../licensing/), [Tools And Solution Packs](../community/tools/), and [Sample And Data Sources](../community/data-sources/).
